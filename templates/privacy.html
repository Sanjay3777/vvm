Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to operate the service: to authenticate you, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you request. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect the security of the service and its users.

4. Data Security

Passwords are stored in hashed form, never in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Your Rights

You can view and edit your expense data at any time, and you may request deletion of your account and all associated data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly means you accept the updated policy.
